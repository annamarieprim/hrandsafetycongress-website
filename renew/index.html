Why RENEW

A morning for the people who take care of everyone else

More than another conference: a morning to invest in the people who invest in others.

RENEW is a morning leadership experience for HR professionals in San Antonio and the surrounding region, focused on reflection, connection, growth and practical takeaways you can bring back to your organization.

Every seat benefits Blood Cancer United, funding research, advocacy and support for patients and families. RENEW is a signature event of For the Kids, Alan Derek Utley’s 2027 Blood Cancer United Visionaries of the Year campaign, presented in partnership with HR + Safety CongressTM.

Meet the Speakers

The Host

Steve Multer

Storytelling Strategist & Keynote Speaker

Steve Multer is a 25-year veteran market messaging strategist and corporate spokesman trusted by Fortune leaders like Cisco, Panasonic, Fujifilm, HP, Siemens, Intel, and over 150 others. He has crafted and delivered more than 15,000 presentations in 38 states and 23 countries, and coached 3,500 speakers with his Story Moves the Needle™ framework. His bestselling book, Nothing Gets Sold Until the Story Gets Told, is a masterclass on winning communication and the neuroscience behind how speakers deliver meaningful value for any audience.

Keynote Speaker

Nicole Bianchi

Keynote Speaker and Best-Selling Author

Nicole Bianchi embodies brave leadership, bold vision, and boundless heart. As the founder of Bravership® (Bravery + Leadership), Nicole has spent her life helping leaders rise — from inside the boardroom leading transformations in Fortune 500 companies to global stages and audiences, her message has become a movement. Not by playing it safe, but by choosing courage over comfort, clarity over chaos, and purpose over fear. Nicole is a truth-teller, a fire-starter, and a fiercely compassionate guide for leaders and teams who are ready to do the inner work that fuels outer impact. Recognized for transforming leadership and team dynamics through her engaging approach and actionable strategies, Nicole inspires leaders to foster a culture of bravery and authenticity by turning tough conversations into opportunities for growth, and using bravery as a competitive advantage. When the stakes are high and brave leadership is the difference between misalignment and momentum, Nicole is the speaker they call to turn insight into action, and action into measurable results.
