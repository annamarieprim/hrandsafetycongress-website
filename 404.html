Recertification credit information

Earn SHRM & HRCI credits

Credits are based on actual instructional time, not breaks or welcomes, and are listed with each eligible event. Attendees who complete the program receive a certificate of attendance with the credit details.

SHRM PDCs

HR + Safety Congress is recognized by SHRM to offer Professional Development Credits (PDCs) for SHRM-CP® or SHRM-SCP® recertification activities.

The SHRM Activity ID is shared with attendees after they complete the program.

Visit shrm.org

HRCI credit hours

HR + Safety Congress is an HRCI® Recertification Provider.

When a program is pre-approved, its HRCI credit hours and credit type are listed on that event’s page. Your Program ID is on your certificate of completion. Enter it in your HRCI recertification application once, within your cycle dates, and keep the certificate for audit.

Visit hrci.org

Safety professionals

BCSP certificants (CSP, ASP, SMS, CHST, OHST, STS) can self-report safety-related conference attendance toward recertification: 1 point per CEU, or 0.5 points per conference day of 6+ hours when CEUs are not awarded, for programs that are at least 50% safety-related.

Keep your certificate of attendance as documentation. Requirements vary, so confirm eligibility with your certifying body.
