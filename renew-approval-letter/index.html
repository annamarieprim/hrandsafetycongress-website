The letter

[Your name]
[Your title, department]
[Date]

Dear [Manager’s name],

I’m requesting approval to attend RENEW., a half-day leadership experience presented by HR + Safety CongressTM on Friday, May 21, 2027, at The Whitley Event Center in San Antonio. The cost is a $150 ticket and one morning out of the office.

Why it matters now. Leaders are under real strain. Gallup’s State of the Global Workplace report found that manager engagement fell from 30% to 27% in 2024, the main reason global employee engagement dropped to 21%. When leaders run on empty, their teams feel it. RENEW. is built to help leaders reset, reconnect and come back with practical next steps.

What I’ll bring back. The program is designed around reflection, connection and growth:

  • Steve Multer (host and opening keynote), a storytelling strategist who has coached 3,500 speakers, on communicating so people act.
  • Nicole Bianchi (closing keynote), founder of Bravership®, on brave leadership and turning tough conversations into growth.
  • Dr. Laura Suttin, a physician and former healthcare executive, on the conditions that drive burnout and attrition.
  • Lauren Costley, CPC, and Alan Derek Utley, on reputation, team strength and healthier workplace cultures.
  • Guided breathing and reflection moments, plus networking with leaders from across the San Antonio region during breakfast, breaks and the exhibitor hall.

1 hour of recertification credit is being applied for.

Cost and time away. Registration is $150, with no travel or hotel costs. The event runs 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM (doors open 7:30 AM), parking is free, and I can be back at work for the mid-afternoon. [I’ll cover / I’ve arranged coverage for] my responsibilities that morning.

A cause our company can be proud of. Every RENEW. ticket purchase is donated to Blood Cancer United through the For the Kids campaign, which funds blood cancer research and support for patients and families.

How I’ll share it. Within a week of the event, I’ll share a one-page summary and [lead a 15-minute team debrief] with three ideas we can put to work right away.

Tickets go on sale April 1, 2027, at hrandsafetycongress.com. With your approval, I’ll register that day to secure my seat.

Thank you for considering this investment in our leadership.

Sincerely,
[Your name]
